# v0.5.57 — MQTT outbound QoS 2

## Goal

A subscriber that asks for QoS 2 receives a QoS 2 PUBLISH and
can finish PUBREC → PUBREL → PUBCOMP without blocking the
publisher when the window is full.

## Performance

No extra allocations. Fanout copies the encoded PUBLISH under
the session lock, then `send()` after unlock. PUBREL is four
bytes, encoded only after a matching PUBREC.

## Security

Grant is clamped to 2. Unknown packet ids are no-ops. A QoS 1
PUBACK cannot free a QoS 2 slot.
