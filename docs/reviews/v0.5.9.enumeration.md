## P1 — Multi-threaded accept loop

- **gap** → `src/server/cmq_server.c:712, 7069` (single listen fd
  registered with the ev_loop; accept_cb runs on the ev_loop
  thread). High connect rates bottleneck.
- **impact**: 100K+ conn/sec can't be served.
- **effort**: L (multi-threaded accept + per-conn handoff).

## P1 — Multi-listener runtime accept loop

- **gap** → `src/server/cmq_server.c:7027` (cmq_server_run binds
  only `listen_fds[0]`; slots[1..3] are reserved but never bound).
- **impact**: cannot serve two distinct certs/ports from one process.
- **effort**: M (200 LOC: iterate slots, per-listener accept_cb).

## P1 — WS permessage-deflate

- **gap** → `src/server/cmq_ws.c` (extend WS handshake to negotiate
  `permessage-deflate; server_no_context_takeover`; per-conn z_stream).
- **impact**: bandwidth waste on WS clients.
- **effort**: L.

## P1 — 5.0 wildcard PUBACK match
