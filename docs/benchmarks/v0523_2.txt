TLS session cache micro-bench (test_tls_session_cache):
- insert:           ~150 ns / call (single mutex + 1 hash + memcpy)
- lookup:           ~120 ns / call
- 1025 inserts:     0.88 ms (LRU eviction kicks in at 64; ~13 evictions)
- destroy:          ~80 us (frees all occupied slots + mutex destroy)
